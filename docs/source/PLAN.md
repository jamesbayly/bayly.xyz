## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Personas

**Persona A: The Startup Founder (Seed–Series A)**
- Needs someone to build their AI product from prototype to production
- Can't afford/find a full CTO but needs technical leadership
- Typically B2B SaaS, AI-enabled, or web3
- Cares about: speed to market, production experience, cost efficiency

**Persona B: The Scale-Up CPO/CTO (Series A–B)**
- Has a team but needs a senior product/tech leader for a specific initiative
- Needs someone who can take a messy concept and ship it
- Often in AI/ML, compliance, or blockchain infrastructure
- Cares about: technical depth, product strategy, delivery track record

**Persona C: The Agency Lead**
- Needs a senior contractor for a client engagement
- Needs someone who can integrate into a team and deliver
- Cares about: reliability, communication, professional references

**Persona D: The Government Innovation Lead**
- Needs a consultant to build AI capability, governance, and transformation strategy
- NZ public service, Crown entities, SOEs
- Cares about: process maturity, governance frameworks, Te Tiriti, procurement, risk

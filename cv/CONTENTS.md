## 6. Selected Work

### Porticus | Founder

**Building an AI-native universal compliance platform.**

I founded Porticus to help organisations manage multiple compliance frameworks without duplicating the same controls, documents and evidence across disconnected systems.

The platform ingests standards, legislation, certification schemes, contracts and internal policies, then turns them into structured compliance programmes. Its knowledge graph maps equivalent, overlapping and conflicting obligations across frameworks so evidence can be provided once and reused wherever it applies.

I own the company strategy, product, architecture, engineering, UX, pricing, partnerships and customer delivery. The technical platform uses TypeScript, Azure Functions, Azure SQL, Event Grid, OpenAI and Azure AI, with structured outputs, confidence controls, traceability and human-review gates.

Porticus is built for compliance-heavy SMB and mid-market organisations, initially in New Zealand and Australia, with a focus on food production and export, manufacturing and consultant-led compliance services.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** AI product founding, knowledge graphs, structured LLM pipelines, compliance domain modelling, event-driven architecture, enterprise UX, partner-led go-to-market and commercial contracting.

### SubQuery and OnFinality | Chief Product Officer

**From seed stage to a globally recognised infrastructure business.**

I led product across two developer-focused blockchain infrastructure businesses, owning product strategy, roadmaps, delivery, commercial growth and public representation.

Over four years I helped take SubQuery through seed funding, product-market fit, a token generation event and public listing on major exchanges. I led more than $20M in capital raising across three rounds and managed investor relationships with venture and strategic investors.

At peak, I led a global team of more than 40 people across New Zealand, Singapore and Europe. We supported thousands of development teams and built a community of more than 200,000 people.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** executive product leadership, capital raising, distributed team building, developer platforms, enterprise partnerships, go-to-market, public communication and full startup lifecycle execution.

### 8ai | Co-founder and CTO

**A production AI customer-service product built from zero to revenue.**

I co-founded and built an AI support platform serving customers across New Zealand, Australia, Asia and the United Kingdom.

The product handled customer conversations across chat, email and WhatsApp using multi-agent workflows and RAG over customer knowledge bases. I led the architecture, engineering, product roadmap, deployment, operations and customer conversations.

The company joined Entrepreneur First in London and reached positive cashflow with a lean team.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** AI product development, RAG, multi-agent systems, React, TypeScript, Node.js, product operations, customer development and founder-led sales.

### AI Transformation and Governance | Confidential NZ organisations

**Practical AI transformation for government and high-trust environments.**

I developed an AI transformation and enablement approach focused on process and service improvement, not generic productivity training.

The work included an opportunity-assessment rubric, maturity diagnostic, six-pillar governance and operating model, procurement and model-selection frameworks, assurance artefacts, incident response, workforce enablement and a sequenced implementation roadmap.

The approach accounted for New Zealand public-sector expectations, privacy, explainability, equity, Te Tiriti and Māori data sovereignty, alongside ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** AI strategy, governance, assurance, procurement, operating-model design, public-sector alignment and implementation planning.

### Sentro | Technical Product Leadership and Full-Stack Engineering

**Product delivery in a regulated insurance environment.**

I first worked with Sentro as Technical Project Manager, leading the design and delivery of its group-insurance policy administration platform. I owned the delivery pipeline from solution design and requirements through UX, development, testing and deployment, coordinating internal and external engineering teams.

I later returned as a full-stack engineering consultant, delivering production features across .NET, Angular and React within an Azure-hosted, ISO 27001-certified environment.
